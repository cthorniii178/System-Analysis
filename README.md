# DriverPass Project Reflection

## Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client for the project was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help customers better prepare for their driving tests. They wanted a system that would allow customers to create accounts, purchase training packages, take practice tests, and schedule driving lessons. The system also needed to support employees and instructors who would manage appointments, customer information, training, and other parts of the business.

## What did you do particularly well?

I think I did particularly well breaking the requirements into smaller parts and showing how they would work together. Creating the different UML diagrams helped me take the client's requirements and turn them into a system design that could be understood by both the client and a development team. I also think I did well considering how customers and employees would interact with the system instead of only focusing on the technical side.

## If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve some of my UML diagrams. As I became more familiar with UML throughout the course, I gained a better understanding of how much detail should be included and how relationships and processes should be represented. I would review my earlier diagrams and make some of the relationships, labels, and decision points clearer and more consistent.

## How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the DriverPass requirements and identifying what customers, employees, instructors, and administrators needed to accomplish with the system. I then used those needs to determine the processes and information that needed to be represented in my system design. For example, my activity diagram for scheduling a driving lesson includes checking the customer's active package and remaining lesson hours, selecting the preferred appointment information, and checking trainer and vehicle availability before reserving the lesson. Considering user needs is important because even if a system technically works, it will not be successful if it does not solve the problems of the people who actually use it.

## How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

My approach to designing software is to first understand the problem and what the client expects the system to accomplish. From there, I would identify the users, requirements, major processes, and information the system needs. I would continue using techniques such as reviewing requirements, creating UML diagrams, breaking larger processes into smaller steps, and getting feedback throughout the design process. I would also make sure the documentation stays updated because clear documentation makes it easier for other developers and team members to understand the design and make changes in the future.
